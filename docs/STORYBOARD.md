# Storyboard — "One grid, twelve energy stories"

**Audience:** regional planners, energy-transition advisers, curious citizens.
**Core question:** is France's low-carbon electricity the same story in every region?
**Single message:** the mix, carbon intensity and self-sufficiency of regions diverge strongly — so does the effort still needed.

| # | Section | Headline (one sentence) | Visuals / interactions | Question answered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook & context | The grid is national, the energy stories are regional. | Text, 3 info cards, definitions expander | Why should I care? What will I learn? |
| 2 | The big picture | Most production is low-carbon, with a seasonal carbon rhythm. | 5 KPIs with Δ vs previous 12 months; stacked area (mix); 2 annotated lines | How green is the selection, and is it improving? |
| 3 | Regions side by side | Regions differ widely on the chosen metric. | Bubble map; ranking bar with average line; small multiples of mix by year; box plots; month × region heatmap | Who leads, who lags, how stable, which season? |
| 4 | What if…? | Where would more wind/solar or less thermal help most? | 3 sliders; before/after bars (CO₂ and coverage) | Where does effort pay off? |
| 5 | Can we trust the data? | Checks mostly pass; limits are explained. | Check table, missingness heatmap, cleaning & limits text | How reliable is this? |
| 6 | Insights & next steps | Data-driven take-aways and implications. | Computed insights (change with filters) | What should we do next? |

**Interaction design:** sidebar filters (regions, dates, metric, thermal emission factor) change every chart and KPI; the what-if sliders are the only in-page controls.
**Accessibility:** colour-blind-safe palette, units on all axes, text description under each chart.
